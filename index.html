Respect and responsibility

Learning with respect for Country and community.

We acknowledge the Wurundjeri People of the Kulin Nation, Traditional Owners of the lands on which Swinburne operates, and pay our respects to Elders past and present. We recognise the continuing connection of Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander peoples to Country, culture and knowledge.

We are committed to listening to First Nations educators and communities and to supporting learning technology that respects their knowledge, priorities and self-determination. We encourage Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander people to apply for our roles.
