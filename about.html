Respect and responsibility

Learning with respect for home, heritage and community.

We acknowledge the diverse ethnic communities and Indigenous peoples of Vietnam, whose deep connection to land, culture, and knowledge has shaped the country for generations. We recognise the continuing importance of heritage, identity, and community in building a respectful learning environment.

We are committed to listening to local educators, communities, and cultural leaders, and to supporting learning technology that reflects their values, traditions, and perspectives. We encourage people from all backgrounds across Vietnam to contribute and participate in our work.
